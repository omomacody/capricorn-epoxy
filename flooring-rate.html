  • Existing floor condition
  • Cracks, damage, or uneven surfaces
  • Surface preparation requirements
  • Floor size and layout
  • Epoxy system required
  • Design and finish
  • Primer and material requirements
  • Number of coats
  • Location of the project
  • Accessibility and site conditions
  • Additional work required

Before You Send Your Floor Video

To help our team analyse your project accurately, please follow these instructions:

  • Hold your phone vertically (9:16).
  • Record the entire floor area slowly.
  • Make sure the floor is clearly visible.
  • Show damaged, cracked, uneven, or wet areas.
  • Avoid very dark or blurry footage.
  • Record in good daylight whenever possible.
  • Show the actual condition of the project area.
Important: Please do not send edited or heavily filtered videos. We need to see the actual condition of the floor for our preliminary assessment.
