📹 Before Sending Your Video

In tih duhna lai hmun in chhuat kha video-in chiang deuhin lo cover kual hmasa rawh le. Please record a clear video of your floor before continuing to WhatsApp.

  • I phone kha a tung zawngin keng la. Hold your phone vertically (9:16).
  • Muangchangin i chhuat video kha record kual la. Slowly record the entire floor area.
  • Chhuat kha chiang takin ti lang kim la. Make sure the floor is clearly visible.
  • Chhuat khi emaw bumboh deuh te pawh ti lang la. Show cracks, damaged or uneven areas.
  • Hmun thim lutuk emaw video fiah lo lutuk tur kha veng la. Avoid dark or blurry footage.
  • Video ah khan filter hman loh tur. Do not heavily edit or filter the video.
